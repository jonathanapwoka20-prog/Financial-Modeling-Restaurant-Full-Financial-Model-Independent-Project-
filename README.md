# Integrated 3-Statement Financial Model – Restaurant Concept

## Project Overview
**Goal:** Developed a high-fidelity, dynamic 3-year financial model for a restaurant concept preparing for a seed funding round. This model serves as a strategic decision-making tool for stakeholders to assess valuation, operational feasibility, and investment risk.

* **Project Type:** Independent Financial Modeling & Forecasting
* **Role:** Lead Financial Analyst (End-to-End Development)
* **Tools Used:** Advanced Excel (INDEX/MATCH, Scenario Manager, Data Validation, Integrated Linking)
* **Model Horizon:** 3-Year Monthly & Annual Projections

---

## Context and Challenge
The challenge was to move beyond simple "percentage growth" forecasting and build a **bottom-up operational model**. The goal was to ensure the model was audit-ready, structurally sound, and capable of withstanding stress testing across various market scenarios.

### Project Objectives Demonstrated:
* **Bottom-Up Revenue Modeling:** Linking physical capacity (table turnover, seat count) to financial outcomes.
* **Full Financial Integration:** Achieving a perfectly balanced Balance Sheet linked to P&L and Cash Flow.
* **Risk Assessment:** Utilizing sensitivity analysis to measure the impact of fluctuating rent, labor costs, and RCF (Revolving Credit Facility) utilization.

---

## The Action Taken (Model Architecture)

### A. Integrated Model Structure
I built a fully linked 3-statement model using a modular approach to ensure scalability:
* **Income Statement:** Includes detailed COGS and OPEX breakdown.
* **Balance Sheet:** Perfectly balanced using a "Cash-as-a-plug" method or RCF logic.
* **Cash Flow Statement:** Built via the indirect method to reconcile net income to cash on hand.

### B. Detailed Supporting Schedules
To drive the core statements, I developed the following granular schedules:
* **Revenue & Cost Drivers:** Sales modeled based on operational assumptions like **Average Check Size** and **Table Turnover Rate**.
* **Fixed Assets Schedule:** Calculated monthly depreciation and CAPEX requirements.
* **Working Capital:** Dynamic modeling of accounts receivable, inventory, and payables.
* **Capital Structure:** Modeled debt repayment and equity injections.

### C. Advanced Financial Metrics
* **DCF Valuation:** Calculated Enterprise Value using the Discounted Cash Flow method.
* **Return Metrics:** Built-in Internal Rate of Return (IRR) and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) for investor review.
* **HR Schedule:** A segregated labor schedule for wage growth, benefits, and payroll taxes.
